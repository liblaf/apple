The loss curve keeps the handoff honest

The lower panel joins the shared prefix to its independent release. It shows a genuine warm start: the release begins exactly at the saved shared final state, then improves. It does not establish convergence.

What “shared” means

One activation for every muscle triangle

The shared run uses one symmetric 2D activation vector for all 400 muscle triangles. This square is the affine deformation preferred by that activation alone, not the equilibrium deformation of the pork body.

Interpretation boundary

What this page does and does not compare

Included: exactly matched saved h=.20, ν=.49 band-model histories and their material-only renders.

Not available: an exact ν=.35 h=.20 history. It is shown as missing, not inferred from a different height or geometry.

Not a convergence claim: all included histories failed the strict inverse-stationarity gate and their refinement restarted/stalled. The extreme deformation and folding are observations requiring further constrained or regularized studies, not evidence of physical muscle behavior.
