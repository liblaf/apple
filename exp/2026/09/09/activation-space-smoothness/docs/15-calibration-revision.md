# Learned-axis calibration revision after the initial grid failed

Every candidate in the original bounded learned-axis grid, including its predeclared half-rate fallback, was ineligible. The run ended with an explicit calibration failure at 04:14 on September 9. This revision changes calibration before any primary fit. The three planned fits, seed 20260909, initialization strength, C-smoothness objective, solver tolerances, primary update budgets, failure policy, and final deadline remain as approved in [the study plan](12-learned-axis-plan.md).

The original first-step physical matching produced rates 62.863886, 106.581002, and 180.005509. The first completed pilot's fit worsened from 5.30993 mm to 18.09783 mm; the second failed its equilibrium solve; the third ended at 227.38503 mm. The half-rate pilot, 31.431943, ended at 8.57352 mm. All four completed selection receipts report `stable: false`.

Matching the first change in Z was insufficient for v coordinates near the small nonzero initialization. The C map is quadratic in v, and its derivative changes as v grows. In the first pilot the fitting-gradient RMS rose from 3.06×10⁻⁶ at initialization to 4.47×10⁻² at update three. The observed multistep trajectories, rather than inversion counts or a newly imposed activation cap, motivate the lower search bracket.

Run exactly four fresh 16-update fit-only pilots at the original smallest rate divided by 64, 32, 16, and 8: approximately 0.982248, 1.964496, 3.928993, and 7.857986. The grid is closed. Require finite fields and successful forward/adjoint solves throughout, positive fitting progress, and final fit loss within 1% of the best post-update loss. Select the smallest rate within 5% of the largest eligible fractional fit improvement. If none passes, record the failure without substituting another rate.

At the selected 16-update endpoint, recompute λ₀ = 0.25 ‖∇ᵥL_fit‖/‖∇ᵥS_C‖. Test exactly three fresh 16-update pilots at λ₀/4, λ₀, and 4λ₀. Retain the original total-objective stability, 80% fitting-progress retention, 90% incremental target-projection retention, and positive S_C-reduction requirements, evaluated against the fit-only pilot at update 16. Choose the smallest eligible coefficient within five percentage points of the largest S_C reduction. No automatic fallback is added.

This costs 119 nominal evaluated states, estimated at about 35 minutes from observed timings. The previously passed initial equivalence and derivative checks are reused because the model and initialization are unchanged. The original grid and failed states remain in `data/18-learned-axis-calibration-v2/`; new evidence goes to `data/18-learned-axis-calibration-refined/`. Pilot controls and moments are discarded before the primary fits. A 16-update screen is not a guarantee of 256-update stability; the declared primary failure policy and 128-update milestone remain in force.
