## Field definition and alignment

For every state, the principal mode is the **largest algebraic eigenvalue** \(z_1\) of \(Z=BB^T-I\), with its unoriented reference eigenaxis \(n_1\). Each centered segment lies at the deformed tetrahedron centroid and points along \(\operatorname{normalize}(F n_1)\).

Color encodes

$$
100\,\operatorname{sign}(z_1)\left(1-\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+|z_1|}}\right),
$$

on the shared signed scale [-100%, 100%]. Segment length is 4.5 mm times the absolute display magnitude divided by 100. These are effective activation modes, not displacement vectors or measured anatomical fibers. The principal eigenvalues in these five saved states are nonnegative, so the blue half of the signed palette is unused; residual modes of the full tensor are not shown.

Every shape uses its complete saved activation. In particular, the free-activation shape is generated by the full tensor even though only its principal mode is visualized below it. The dominant-only tensor matches the positive principal projection of the free tensor to maximum absolute error **1.42 × 10⁻¹⁴**. Their reference principal fields therefore agree, while their equilibrated shapes and transported glyph directions can differ.

The same current-tensor eligibility rule is applied to every column: suppress neutral modes below \(|z_1|=10^{-8}\) and axes whose normalized leading eigenvalue gap is at most \(10^{-6}\). This differs slightly from the previous fixed-axis figure, which also filtered using ambiguity of the original full-tensor source axes. The common current-tensor rule restores 17 formerly suppressed fixed directions and suppresses 11 very weak directions, a net change of six lines before visibility filtering. Source-axis ambiguity remains an interpretation limit.

Visibility is recomputed on each deformed muscle-region geometry using the same front-region-label rule. The view is a dense region-context display, including eligible interior tetrahedra; differing visible-line counts do not indicate identical visible tetrahedron sets across states. All active-cell glyphs and deformed skin surfaces are also exported as VTP files.

## Validation and observations

The input audit confirmed identical rest points and 288,235 active tetrahedron IDs across the five states. The renderer independently reconstructed tensors, fit RMS and deformation determinants. The maximum normalized principal-eigenpair residual is **1.82 × 10⁻¹⁵**. Exact equality of camera position, focal point, up vector, parallel scale, and the x/y/w screen-projection rows is asserted across all shape and field panels within each view. All **34 PNGs** reopen successfully; the two comparison plates were visually inspected.

The dominant-only shape loses expression amplitude; scalar refitting recovers much of it. Fixed and released fields retain organized directional patterns, while the scratch field is visibly less coherent. The released and scratch shapes show more local irregularity, and their volume meshes contain inversions. The lower scratch fitting error should therefore be read together with its geometry diagnostics. These images support a descriptive comparison rather than an isolated causal conclusion about initialization.
