## Physical model

The repaired constitutive coordinates `X` come from `reference-clearance-002`. The authoritative loaded neutral is `X + u0`, saved in `forward-isfixed-001`. Disabling collision changes the force balance. Each expression starts by re-equilibrating the saved neutral with collision disabled and records that state separately in `collision-off-start.npz` and `collision-off-start.json`. The transferred target geometry and constitutive reference remain unchanged.

The model uses multiplicative active strain with six unrestricted symmetric Raw6 entries per retained active tetrahedron, jointly optimized with a six-coordinate rigid mandible pose. Skin pre-strain, modulus, and thickness are checked against the corrected-neutral arrays exactly. Original FEM nodes are fixed only by `IsFixed`; all 3,408 `IsLip` nodes remain free. Only the 2,249 all-original-IsFixed tetrahedra are excluded from bulk assembly. Source cell IDs and retained-local activation rows are recorded separately.

The numerical model has `collision=None`. Forward energy, residual, Hessian, material pullback, and trial admission therefore have no IPC contribution. The hybrid solver uses an unconditional unit admission fraction instead of calling IPC CCD. Appended cranium, mandible, and eyeball geometry remains available for display. Contact validity and collision-free claims are inapplicable.

Each accepted forward must satisfy a free-force norm no larger than `1e-8` solver units, or `0.01 N`. The existing allowance is at most 100 inverted retained tetrahedra and at most `1e-4` of retained rest volume. Counts, fraction, and minimum `det(F)` are recorded at every accepted state.

## Optimization and evidence

`10-fit.py` implements the experiment. `collision_off_runtime.py` preserves the optimized PNCG / sparse Newton hybrid, and `collision_off_seed.py` predicts the coupled material and jaw response. Sparse/native Hessian agreement and shifted residuals are checked in the runtime. The initial fixed-state Lagrangian finite differences check Raw6 and all six jaw pullbacks. These checks validate implementation; they do not establish reduced-objective convergence.

The adjoint starts with relative diagonal shift 0.001, explicitly labelled a damped approximation. A ten-update plateau and small unrestricted gradient can produce only `stationarity_candidate_requires_audit`. A fresh lower-shift or unshifted adjoint plus feasible re-equilibrated objective probes are required before claiming inverse convergence. A chunk limit, small step, line-search stall, or inversion boundary is never called convergence.

`40-run-queue.py` alternates the two expressions in immutable chunks, one numerical process at a time. Continuations preserve the exact normalized pose, four Adam moment arrays, separate q/pose counters, and the original convergence reference plus recent accepted history. Normal Cherries profiles use descriptive names/tags with Git commits disabled.

`20-audit.py` independently rebuilds and evaluates an endpoint. `30-review.py` produces the full original FEM boundary with bones/eyes, target comparisons, error maps, and force/fit curves. Endpoint audits certify the forward equilibrium and declared geometry policy, separately from inverse convergence.
